# OmniPress Tax, Legal & Forensic Analysis Skill

Use this skill when drafting content on Tax Law, Fiscal Technology, Court Decisions (STF/STJ/CARF), and Judicial Forensic Reports (*Laudos e Pareceres Periciais*).

## Strict Ethics & Privacy Mandates
- **De-Identification**: Always run through `sanitize_text`. Never disclose lawsuit numbers or party names subject to judicial secrecy (*segredo de justiça*).
- **Objectivity**: Distinguish clearly between statutory facts, technical evidence, and interpretive arguments.

## Target Platform Tuning
1. **Long-Form (Jusbrasil / Substack / Legal Blog)**:
   - Exhaustive legal-technical breakdown.
   - Cross-references to relevant statutes, constitutional articles, and precedent summaries.
2. **LinkedIn**:
   - Executive summary for CFOs, General Counsels, Tax Directors, and Judges.
   - Clear takeaways on fiscal risk, compliance burdens, and strategic defense.
3. **X (Twitter)**:
   - Fast commentary on breaking court decisions and regulatory normative instructions.
4. **Reddit**:
   - Target `r/direito`, `r/contabilidade`, or specialized law communities.

# OmniPress Public Administration & GovTech Skill

Use this skill when drafting content on digital public services, government efficiency, open data, and civic technology.

## Tone & Writing Guidelines
- **Audience**: Public administrators, policy makers, civic technologists, citizens.
- **Voice**: Institutional, transparent, citizen-centric, analytical.
- **Focus Areas**:
  - Digital transformation in municipalities and state organs.
  - Procurement laws and tech modernization (Lei 14.133).
  - Open data, interoperability, and AI governance in the public sector.

## Target Platform Tuning
1. **Long-Form (Medium / Portal de Gestão Pública)**:
   - Case studies on administrative modernization and cost reduction.
2. **LinkedIn**:
   - Institutional authority and best practices for municipal/state leadership.
3. **X & Threads**:
   - High-impact data visualizations and public transparency insights.
